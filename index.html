How it works

  1. Copy the link of any YouTube video.
  2. Paste it above and press Transcribe.
  3. Watch a short ad while your transcript is prepared.
  4. Copy the text or download it as a file.

FAQ

Does it work on every video?

It works on videos that have captions, including auto-generated ones. If a video has none, you'll see a message saying so.

Is it free?

Yes. The ads you see while waiting keep XT Transcribe free.

Which languages are supported?

Any language the video has captions in. If there's more than one, pick it from the list above the transcript.

Do you save my transcripts?

No. Your link is used to fetch the transcript and it's shown only in your browser.

Privacy policy

XT Transcribe doesn't ask you to sign up and doesn't store the links you paste or the transcripts you get.

Our ad partners may use cookies to show ads. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

XT Transcribe isn't affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google.
